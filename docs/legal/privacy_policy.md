## Summary

GPS Camera processes your photos and location on your device. It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Photos are never uploaded by the app. Some optional features send coordinates or search text to online services, only after you allow online services.

## Data processed on your device

- Photos you take or import, their thumbnails and edited copies, stored in the app's private storage.
- Location from your device's location service (latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, speed and heading when available), used to stamp photos and write photo metadata (EXIF).
- Stamp templates, settings, recently used locations and cached addresses, stored in a local database on your device.

## Location access

- Location is requested only "while using the app". The app does not request or use background location.
- Location tracking pauses when the app is not on screen.
- You can take photos without granting location access, and you can choose a location manually on the map instead.

## Online services (optional, with your consent)

When you allow online services in the app (Settings › Privacy › Online location services), the following data is sent:

- Reverse geocoding (address lookup): the photo or device coordinates are sent to the selected provider – your device's geocoder (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) or OpenStreetMap Nominatim (or another Nominatim-compatible service configured by the publisher).
- Place search: the text you type in the map search is sent to the same provider.
- Map display and map snapshots: map tiles for the area you view are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers (or the configured tile provider). These requests reveal the approximate area being viewed.
- Weather: rounded coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to obtain current weather.

These providers receive your device's IP address as part of the network request and process it under their own privacy policies. No photos, file names or personal identifiers are sent. You can withdraw consent at any time; the camera, GPS coordinates, stamps and metadata editing keep working offline.

## Photo metadata

- New photos can contain GPS coordinates and time in their EXIF metadata (Settings › Gallery & metadata › Save location in photo metadata).
- When sharing, you can remove GPS metadata from the shared copy. A visible stamp on the image itself is not removed by this option.

## Sharing and device gallery

- Photos leave the app only when you share or export them, or when you enable saving to the device gallery.
- Shared files are handled by the app you choose to share with.

## Permissions

- Camera – to take photos.
- Location (while in use) – to add location information.
- Photo library (add / read) – to save photos to your gallery and import photos you select.

## Data retention and deletion

- Data stays on your device until you delete it. Delete individual photos in the gallery, clear cached location data in Settings › Privacy, or uninstall the app to remove all app data.
- Photos already saved to your device gallery or shared elsewhere are not removed by uninstalling.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children and does not transmit personal data to the publisher.

## Changes

The publisher may update this policy. The current version is included in the app.

## Contact

For questions about privacy, use the support contact shown in Settings › About › Contact & support.
